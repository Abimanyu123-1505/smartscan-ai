# SmartScan AI

**Uncertainty-Aware Predictive Frequency-Time Scheduling for Electronic Support**

SIH 2026 — Problem Statement 26055

## Overview
SmartScan AI introduces a novel approach to predictive spectrum monitoring in environments with constrained receiver resources. In electronic support scenarios, wideband environments often exceed the instantaneous bandwidth of available receivers, making full observability impossible. SmartScan addresses this by intelligently scheduling receiver tuning across frequency and time to maximize information gain and probability of intercept.

This research focuses heavily on empirical validation using real-world RF data rather than purely synthetic models. By leveraging real over-the-air recordings, SmartScan accounts for the true complexity of wireless propagation, including unpredictable channel fading, coexistence interference, and non-ideal hardware effects that are often missed in simulated environments.

## Research Claim
SmartScan AI demonstrates that uncertainty-aware, predictive tuning policies (utilizing models like UCB, Thompson Sampling, and sequential predictors) achieve significantly higher probability of intercept and spectral awareness in partially observable wideband environments compared to traditional sequential scanning or random hopping baselines, when evaluated on real-world RF measurements.

## Real Data Setup
The system is designed to use real RF datasets.
1. View the dataset registry in `data/datasets.yaml`
2. Follow the specific instructions provided in the registry to download the required datasets (e.g., from Zenodo, IEEE DataPort, or AERPAW).
3. Place downloaded files into the `data/raw/` directory.

Note: The `data/raw/` directory is gitignored to prevent large files from being committed to the repository.

To process raw data, run the ingestion pipeline:
```bash
python -m smartscan.backend.ingest --dataset <dataset_id>
```

## Architecture
```
[Real Data / Environment] ---> [ReplayEngine] ---> [ReplayReceiver]
                                                          |
                                                          v
[Evaluator] <--- [Policy] <--- [BeliefState] <--- [Observations]
```

- **ReplayEngine**: Manages the playback of real RF recordings, acting as the ground-truth environment.
- **ReplayReceiver**: Simulates a constrained hardware receiver that can only observe a limited bandwidth at any given time slot.
- **BeliefState**: Maintains the probabilistic assessment of spectrum occupancy across all bands based on historical observations.
- **Policy**: The decision-making agent that selects which frequency band to monitor next (e.g., Random, Sequential, UCB, Reinforcement Learning).
- **Evaluator**: Measures performance metrics (e.g., probability of intercept, regret) by comparing the policy's actions against the hidden ground truth.

This separation strictly prevents data leakage; the Policy only has access to the constrained Observations provided by the ReplayReceiver, ensuring a realistic evaluation of partial observability.

## Limitations
- Datasets are civilian/ISM/community due to the unavailability of classified military datasets.
- Emitter-level ground truth labels are often imperfect or derived from energy thresholds rather than true source annotations.
- Evaluation relies on the counterfactual assumption that the observer does not affect the environment (valid for passive sensing).
- Certain hardware effects (like tuning delay or LO leakage) are currently simplified or not fully modelled in the ReplayReceiver.
