SIH 2026 · Problem Statement 26055

Adaptive Predictive Frequency-Time
Scheduling for Electronic Support

SmartScan AI optimizes narrowband receiver sensing under strict partial observability—predicting emitter activity, tracking Bayesian model uncertainty, and minimizing interception latency over real RF recordings.

⚠️ Problem Statement

Narrowband receivers have limited instantaneous bandwidth ($B_{\text{instant}} \ll B_{\text{total}}$). Traditional sequential scanning cycles blindly ($F_1 \to F_2 \to \dots \to F_N$), suffering high intercept latency and missing short bursty signals.

✨ SmartScan Solution

SmartScan evaluates candidate frequencies using a multi-objective utility function blending predicted activity, Beta variance uncertainty, autocorrelation periodicity, and tuning switch costs—operating under strict partial observability.
