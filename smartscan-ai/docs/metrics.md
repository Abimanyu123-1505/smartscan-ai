# Metrics

Our primary evaluation metrics are:
- **Detection Accuracy**: The ratio of correctly identified signal categories to the total samples.
- **Scheduling Latency**: The turnaround time from data ingestion to a scheduling decision.
- **Spectral Efficiency**: A synthetic measure of throughput based on the simulated clean channel assignments.
- **Data Leakage Score**: Ensures 0% overlap between train and test datasets.
