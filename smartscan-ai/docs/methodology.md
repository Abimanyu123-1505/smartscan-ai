# Methodology

Our methodology integrates statistical signal processing with deep learning.

- **Signal Processing**: Uses short-time Fourier transforms (STFT) and energy detection for baseline assessments.
- **Deep Learning**: Deploys CNNs and Transformers to classify spectral signatures.
- **Reinforcement Learning**: (Optional) For the scheduling module, allowing adaptive assignment to simulated channels (F1..FN) based on ongoing environmental metrics.
